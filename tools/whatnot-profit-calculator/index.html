How the spreadsheet works

  1. Settings: pick your market, the tier Seller Hub shows you this period, your main category and the day your 4-week period started.
  2. Inventory: one row per item or lot you buy. Unit cost, quantity sold, quantity left and stock value calculate.
  3. Shows: one row per live show with its category, hours live and giveaway or other costs.
  4. Sales: one row per order: show, SKU, sale price, buyer shipping and tax. The commission rate comes from the show's category (live) or the item's category (marketplace) at your tier. Every row says OK or what to fix.
  5. Dashboard: gross sales, fees, cost of goods, net profit and margin, profit by month, stock on hand and the tier you're on pace for.

Whatnot fees the spreadsheet uses

Commission is a percentage of the item's sale price only, not buyer shipping or tax. Since 21 September 2026 the rate depends on your category and on your tier, which is set by your total sales in a 4-week period and applies to the next period.

Payment processing is charged on the whole order the buyer pays (item, shipping and tax) plus a fixed fee: 2.9% + $0.30 in the US, Canada and Australia; 2.42% + £/€0.25 plus VAT in the UK and EU.

FAQ

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it, or use File → Import. Every calculation is a plain formula: no macros, no add-ins, no scripts.

Which Whatnot fees does it include?

Commission at your tier and category (the tiered rates Whatnot introduced on 21 September 2026), payment processing on the order total plus the fixed fee, and VAT on fees for the UK and EU. You can override the rate on any sale for promotions or custom terms.

Which countries are covered?

The rate cards for the United States, Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom, Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, France and Belgium. Japan is not included.

What is the difference between the free Lite sheet and the full spreadsheet?

Lite is one sheet for one show of up to 50 orders with rates you type in. The full spreadsheet adds inventory with cost of goods, any number of shows and marketplace sales, every category's rate at every tier, a 4-week tier tracker, profit by month and row-by-row checks.

Is this affiliated with Whatnot?

No. It is an independent spreadsheet. Whatnot Seller Hub is the source of truth for your fees and payouts.
