The problem

FAQ

Is this affiliated with Faire?

No. It is an independent offline tool. Always verify fees in the Faire Help Center and your Brand Portal Payout Summary.

Do I need to export anything from Faire?

Not for pricing. Enter wholesale, COGS and shipping numbers yourself. To audit an order, copy amounts from your Payout Summary and compare.

Why don't my numbers match an order?

Promotions, replacements, split orders, currency, free-shipping programs or custom terms can differ. Check the region, order type, payout speed and fee constants.

Can I change the fee rates?

Yes, in the Advanced fees panel. Changes are saved in your browser, and Reset to defaults restores the built-in set.

Does it work offline and on iPhone?

Yes. Open the file from Files or AirDrop. No network is needed.
