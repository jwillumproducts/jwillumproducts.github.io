The problem

The same item nets a different amount on Etsy, Whatnot and Faire. Each platform takes its own mix of listing, commission or transaction, and processing fees, and some fees apply to shipping as well as the item price. Comparing them by hand before you set a price is slow.

How it works

  1. Download the free file from Gumroad and open it in Excel, or import it into Google Sheets (File > Import).
  2. On the Fee Calculator tab, type your item price, shipping charged, cost of goods, and your shipping and packaging cost into the blue cells.
  3. For Faire, choose New or Reorder.
  4. Read fees, profit and margin for all three platforms side by side.
  5. If your rates differ, edit them on the Rates tab.

Default rates

The US default rates shipped in the Rates tab. You can edit every one of them.
