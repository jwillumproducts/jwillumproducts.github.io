The problem

At month end you have hundreds of time entries in Toggl Track, Clockify or Harvest and need to bill them in Xero or Wave. Typing invoice lines by hand is slow, and dumping every entry onto an invoice gives the client hundreds of tiny lines.

How it works

  1. Export a detailed time CSV from Toggl Track, Clockify or Harvest.
  2. Drop it into Tracker Invoice Lines; the source tracker is detected.
  3. Choose how to group lines (for example one line per client and project), the rate column or rate card, and the Billable only filter.
  4. Pick the Xero, Wave or Generic preset and check the preview for ERROR and WARN chips.
  5. Download the CSV and import it in Xero (Invoices → Import) or Wave (Wave Connect).

FAQ

Does Tracker Invoice Lines create PDF invoices or email clients?

No. It only turns tracker rows into accounting import line CSVs.

Does it upload to Xero or Wave?

No. There is no OAuth, no API and no network use of your timesheet. You download a CSV and import it yourself.

Why didn't Wave Validate accept my file?

Wave Connect headers come from your business's Prepare Input Sheet (yellow cells are required). Column order is flexible, but heading text must match, so rename the headers to match and validate again.

What if one project has mixed rates?

By default the first rate seen is used and a WARN is raised. Turn on Split mixed rates to get separate lines per rate.

Where are my defaults stored?

Invoice prefix, dates, due days, account and tax codes, preferences and the rate card are stored in localStorage on this browser only.
