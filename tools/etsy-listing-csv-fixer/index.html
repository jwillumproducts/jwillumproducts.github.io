FAQ

Does Etsy Listing CSV Fixer upload or create listings on Etsy?

No. It never talks to Etsy. It only validates and cleans a CSV you already exported, then lets you download a cleaned copy and an issue report.

Will it truncate my titles or tags automatically?

No. Truncation is opt-in and off by default. Safe fixes such as trimming, currency stripping and tag delimiter normalizing always run.

My SKUs look like 1.23E+15. Can it fix them?

That is Excel scientific notation. The tool warns but will not invent the original digits. Re-export the SKU column as text, then drop the CSV again.

Tags use commas and pipes mixed together. What happens?

If a pipe (|) appears it splits on pipes; otherwise it splits on commas. Empty tokens are dropped and duplicates removed.

Image cells are Google Drive or Dropbox share links. Is that a problem?

Usually yes, since those are not direct image URLs. You get a warning; replace them with publicly fetchable image URLs.
