What it checks

  • Required columns present (Journal No., Journal Date, Account Name, Debits, Credits) and a real header row (QuickBooks uses row 1 for mapping)
  • Every journal number balances on its own, to the cent, and has at least 2 lines and one date
  • Lines with both a debit and a credit, no amount, negative amounts, or more than 2 decimals
  • Amounts with $ signs, thousand separators or decimal commas; unquoted commas that shift columns
  • Dates: empty, text, spreadsheet serial numbers, impossible dates, mixed day-first and month-first formats
  • Account names: extra spaces, account numbers in front, sub-accounts without Parent:, names not in your account list (with "did you mean")
  • A/R and A/P lines without a customer or vendor in Name
  • Blank rows, duplicate header rows and files of 1,000 rows or more (QuickBooks imports less than 1,000 rows per file)

FAQ

Is my file uploaded?

No. The file is read in this browser tab. The page sends no data anywhere; you can confirm it in your browser's developer tools (Network tab).

What does the free check show?

How many errors and warnings your file has, its totals, and the full detail for the first 3 problems. The $8 template includes the offline checker that shows every problem and downloads a clean CSV.

Which columns does QuickBooks Online need?

Journal No., Journal Date, Account Name, Debits and Credits. Description, Name (customer/vendor), Class and Location are optional. Lines with the same Journal No. form one entry, and each entry must balance.

Why does QuickBooks say an account is invalid?

The name must match your chart of accounts exactly. Sub-accounts must be written Parent:Sub; using only the sub-account name gives a 'Line Account Invalid' error. Paste your account list above to check every line.

My QuickBooks only offers Spreadsheet Sync for journal entries. Does this help?

Intuit says some accounts only get Spreadsheet Sync, which is part of QuickBooks Online Advanced and Accountant. The same rules apply there: balanced entries, exact account names and real dates. Check your lines here first, then paste them into Spreadsheet Sync's Journal Entries template.
