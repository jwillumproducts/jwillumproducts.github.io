Stripe refunds and chargebacks: how to book them

How to book Stripe refunds and chargebacks (disputes): journal entries for the refund, the disputed amount, the dispute fee, and a won or lost dispute.

Published 30 October 2026

Short answer: a refund is money you chose to give back: Dr Refunds / Cr Stripe Clearing, and the original processing fee generally stays an expense. A chargeback (Stripe calls it a dispute) is money the card network pulls back: Stripe takes the disputed amount and usually a dispute fee from your balance. Book the amount to a separate Disputes or Chargebacks account and the fee to Stripe Fees. If you win, Stripe returns the disputed amount and you reverse the first line.

Steps

  1. Find the rows in your Stripe balance export: refunds, disputes (the amount withdrawn), dispute fees, and dispute reversals (funds returned when you win).
  2. Refund: Dr Refunds (or Sales returns) / Cr Stripe Clearing for the refunded amount. Leave the original fee entry alone.
  3. Dispute opened: Dr Chargebacks / Cr Stripe Clearing for the disputed amount; Dr Stripe Fees / Cr Stripe Clearing for the dispute fee.
  4. Dispute won: Dr Stripe Clearing / Cr Chargebacks for the amount returned. Whether the dispute fee comes back depends on Stripe's current rules for your country, so book only what the export shows.
  5. Dispute lost: nothing more to book; the chargeback and fee stay as losses.
  6. Check that Stripe Clearing is back to zero after each payout.

Worked example (illustrative numbers only)

EventJournalEffect on clearing
Partial refundDr Refunds 20.00 / Cr Clearing 20.00−20.00
Dispute opened on an 80.00 saleDr Chargebacks 80.00 / Cr Clearing 80.00−80.00
Dispute fee (made-up 15.00)Dr Stripe Fees 15.00 / Cr Clearing 15.00−15.00
Dispute won, amount returnedDr Clearing 80.00 / Cr Chargebacks 80.00+80.00

Use the real fee shown in your own export; Stripe's dispute fee varies by country.

Tips

  • Book each event on the date it hits your Stripe balance, which is often a later payout than the sale.
  • Keep chargebacks out of your Refunds account so you can see dispute losses separately.
  • If your Stripe balance can't cover a refund or dispute, Stripe may debit your bank; book that as Dr Stripe Clearing / Cr Bank.

FAQ

Is a chargeback the same as a refund in the books?

No. Both reduce income, but keep chargebacks in a separate account so dispute losses and dispute fees stay visible.

Does Stripe charge a fee for disputes?

Usually yes, a dispute fee per dispute. The amount and whether it is returned when you win depend on your country; check Stripe's disputes documentation and your export.

Where do disputes show up in the Stripe export?

As separate rows with a dispute type or reporting category, plus a dispute reversal row if funds come back.
