How to calculate your Faire wholesale fees and real profit per order

Calculate Faire wholesale fees, payout and real profit per order for new, reorder and Faire Direct orders, with a worked example.

Published 28 September 2026 · Updated 13 October 2026

A $200 wholesale order on Faire doesn't mean $200 in your pocket. What you keep depends on whether it's a retailer's first order, a reorder or a Faire Direct order, how fast you want to be paid, and where your business is based. Then come your own costs. This guide walks through the calculation by hand so you can set wholesale prices that still work after fees.

Fee figures below are the defaults from Faire's Help Center as checked on 2026-09-13. Rates and terms change, so always confirm against the Faire Help Center and your Brand Portal Payout Summary, which is the source of truth. This guide is not affiliated with Faire.

Step 1: Know which order type you're pricing

At the time of writing, for North American (US/Canada) brands:

  • Marketplace, first order from a new retailer: 15% commission on the order subtotal plus a one-time $10 new-retailer fee
  • Marketplace, reorder: 15% commission
  • Faire Direct (orders through your own Faire Direct link): 0% commission, but payment processing still applies

For brands outside North America, a first Marketplace order is 25% (15% + a 10% one-time referral) and reorders are 15%. Faire Direct is again 0% commission.

Step 2: Add the payment processing fee

North America uses tiers based on how fast you're paid:

  • Next day: 3.5% + $0.30
  • 30-day: 2.4% + $0.30
  • 60-day: 1.9% + $0.30

Outside North America, ACH Next Day is 3% of (subtotal + shipping reimbursement), and Net 30 is 0%.

For North America, processing applies to the item subtotal. Some calculators also include shipping reimbursement; choose one method and stick to it.

Step 3: Calculate the payout

Faire's North America payout formula is:

Payout = subtotal + shipping reimbursement − commission − processing fee

Worked example (NA, 30-day payout): $200 wholesale subtotal, $18 shipping reimbursement.

  • First order from a new retailer: commission $30 + new-retailer fee $10 + processing (2.4% × $200 + $0.30 = $5.10) = $45.10 in fees, so payout is $200 + $18 − $45.10 = $172.90
  • Reorder: fees $35.10, payout $182.90
  • Faire Direct: fees $5.10, payout $212.90

Step 4: Subtract your own costs

Payout isn't profit. Take off the cost of goods and what you actually paid to ship:

Profit = payout − COGS − your shipping cost Margin = profit ÷ subtotal

With $80 COGS and $18 actual shipping, the example above gives $74.90 profit on a first order, $84.90 on a reorder and $114.90 through Faire Direct. That's the same products, with very different results.

Step 5: Find your break-even wholesale price

Work backwards from the margin you need. For a new-retailer order, the $10 flat fee hits small orders hardest, so test your minimum order value. Roughly:

Price ≈ (COGS + own shipping − shipping reimbursement + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − commission % − processing % − target margin %)

Step 6: Check your whole catalog

Put every SKU in a sheet (wholesale price, COGS, shipping) and run the formula for new, reorder and Direct orders. Flag anything with a thin or negative margin before a retailer orders it.
