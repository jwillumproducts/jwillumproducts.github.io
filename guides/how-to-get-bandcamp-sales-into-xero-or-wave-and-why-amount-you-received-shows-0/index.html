How to get Bandcamp sales into Xero or Wave (and why "amount you received" shows 0)

Get Bandcamp sales into Xero or Wave, and learn why "amount you received" shows 0 since Enhanced Payments. Step-by-step guide.

Published 28 September 2026 · Updated 13 October 2026

Bandcamp gives you a detailed raw sales report, but it isn't in a shape your accounting software understands. Many artists and labels open it and hit the same puzzle: since Bandcamp's Enhanced Payments change in mid-2024, sale rows often show amount you received = 0. The money hasn't disappeared. It shows up on separate payout rows. Once you understand that, reconciling Bandcamp in Xero or Wave gets much easier.

Here's how to do it by hand.

Step 1: Download the raw data sales report

In Bandcamp go to Tools and download the raw data sales report as CSV, for the period you're closing. Keep an untouched copy. The Bandcamp Help article "Reading your raw data sales report" explains each column.

Step 2: Sort rows by what they are

Filter on the item type column and group rows into:

  • Sales: album, track, package (merch) rows
  • Payouts: money sent to your PayPal or bank
  • Refunds and reversals
  • Other: anything else, which you review by hand

Step 3: Choose your view (bank vs. accrual)

This is the key decision, and it's where most spreadsheets get double-counted:

  • Bank view: book one line per payout, because that's what actually lands in your bank or PayPal. Skip individual sale rows; they're already included in the payout. This is what you match against bank feeds in Xero or Wave.
  • Accrual view: book one line per sale using the net amount column, with refunds and reversals as negative amounts. This shows revenue per release and fee detail, which is useful for a P&L or for paying collaborators.

Don't import both into the same bank account, or your income will be counted twice.

Step 4: Build the lines

Bank view: for each payout row record the date, the amount (amount you received, or net amount if that's empty), a payee such as "Bandcamp", a description like "Bandcamp payout 2026-09-01", and the transaction ID as a reference. If transfer fees appear, add them as separate negative expense lines.

Accrual view: for each sale record the date, artist, item type and name, currency, gross, fees, revenue share and net amount. If the transaction fee shows as *, it hadn't settled when you exported. Leave it blank and note it rather than guessing.

Step 5: Watch the currency

If fans paid in more than one currency, split the sheet by currency before importing into a bank account. Don't mix USD and EUR rows in one statement.

Step 6: Format for your software

  • Xero: use the bank statement import layout (date, amount, payee, description, reference) for the bank view.
  • Wave: match the headings on your Wave Connect input sheet.
  • Accountant: a plain P&L sheet from the accrual view is usually enough.

Import as drafts or unreconciled lines and review before approving.

Step 7: Artist splits (labels and collectives)

If you share income, take each sale's net amount × the artist's percentage. Split sales, not payouts. Make sure every artist on a sale row has a percentage; otherwise a lookup can quietly give them 0%.
