Whatnot fee calculator: how Whatnot fees are calculated

Whatnot fees explained: tiered commission (8% US Standard, 6.67% UK/EU Standard), 2.9% + $0.30 processing on the order total, VAT on fees, worked examples and a free sheet.

Published 30 October 2026 · Updated 15 November 2026

Short answer: Whatnot takes two fees per sale. Commission is a percentage of the item's sale price only. Payment processing is a percentage of the whole order (item plus buyer-paid shipping, plus tax where applicable) plus a fixed amount per order. Since 21 September 2026 commission is tiered: at the Standard tier it is 8% in most US, Canada and Australia categories (4% for Coins) and 6.67% in the UK and EU, and it drops as your 4-week sales grow. Processing is 2.9% + $0.30 in the US, Canada and Australia and 2.42% + £/€0.25 in the UK and EU, with VAT on fees.

Fee figures below are from Whatnot's Seller Fees and Commissions Schedule (page updated 1 November 2026, checked 15 November 2026). Since 21 September 2026 commission is tiered: the rate depends on your category and on your total sales in a 4-week period. Promotions and custom terms vary, so confirm in Seller Hub, which is the source of truth for payouts. Not affiliated with Whatnot.

Fee formula

  1. Commission = your category's rate at your tier × item sale price.
  2. Processing = processing rate × (sale price + buyer shipping + buyer tax where applicable) + fixed fee.
  3. UK/EU only: add VAT on the fees (20% in the UK).
  4. Net payout = sale price − commission − processing (− VAT on fees).
  5. Profit = net payout − cost of goods − supplies − any shipping you pay yourself.

Commission rates by tier (from 21 September 2026)

Your tier is set by total sales across all categories in a 4-week period (private shows, local pickup and cancelled orders don't count) and applies to the next period. The rate on each sale follows the show's category for live sales and the product's category for marketplace sales.

Worked examples (illustrative)

US, Standard tier: $20 sale, buyer pays $5 shipping (order value $25). Commission 8% × $20 = $1.60. Processing 2.9% × $25 + $0.30 = $1.03. Total fees $2.63, net payout $17.37.

UK, Standard tier: £20 sale, buyer pays £4 shipping (order value £24). Commission 6.67% × £20 = £1.33. Processing 2.42% × £24 + £0.25 = £0.83. Fees £2.16 plus 20% VAT £0.43 = £2.59, net payout about £17.41.

Note that a higher buyer shipping charge raises your processing fee but not your commission.

FAQ

How much does Whatnot take per sale?

At the US Standard tier, 8% commission on the sale price in most categories (4% for Coins) plus 2.9% + $0.30 processing on the order total. Higher tiers have lower commission. UK/EU Standard is 6.67% plus 2.42% + £/€0.25, with VAT on fees.

What are the Whatnot commission tiers?

Since 21 September 2026 there are seven tiers, Standard and Tiers 1 to 6. Your total sales in a 4-week period set the tier for the next period. In the US, Tier 1 starts at $15,000 and Tier 6 at $500,000; the lowest public rates are 6.50% for Sports and TCG, 4.50% for Fashion and 3.50% for Coins.

Does Whatnot charge commission on shipping?

No, commission is on the item sale price. Processing is charged on the order total, which includes buyer-paid shipping and tax.
